# Code of Conduct

English | [한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ko.md)

Everyone participating in Viewstem is expected to treat others with respect,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or language. This applies to
issues, pull requests, reviews, project communication, and public activity while
representing the project.

## Expected behavior

Discuss ideas and code without attacking people. Explain disagreements with
evidence, accept corrections, and leave room for different levels of experience.
Respect other people's time, privacy, and stated boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
publication of private information without permission, and retaliation against
someone for reporting a concern are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive behavior
after a request to stop is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Send private reports to [miinhlym@gmail.com](mailto:miinhlym@gmail.com).
Include relevant links or context and how you would prefer to be contacted.
Do not post a sensitive report in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow
[Security](SECURITY.md).

The current maintainer, [@miinhlym](https://github.com/miinhlym), handles reports and
moderation. Report details should be shared only as needed to address the incident;
identifying information should not be published without the reporter's permission.
There is no independent moderation team or guaranteed response deadline.

Depending on severity and repetition, moderation may include requesting a change
in behavior, removing content, a warning, or temporary or permanent restrictions
on participation. Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions. Where
appropriate, the maintainer will explain the decision privately. You may request
reconsideration with additional context through the same email address.
